# Supervisory Control Plane (SCP) Sandbox: Phase 1 Demonstration

**Date:** [Date of Demonstration]
**Location:** [Meeting Location/Virtual Link]
**Duration:** 90 Minutes

## Agenda

**Objective:** To demonstrate the core capabilities of the Supervisory Control Plane and satisfy the entry criteria for Phase 1 of the AI Governance Sandbox program.

---

**(0-10 min) Welcome & Introduction**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Welcome and participant introductions.
    *   Review of the shared goals for the 2026–2028 sandbox program.
    *   Overview of the agenda for the session.
*   **Presenter:** Head of AI Governance

---

**(10-30 min) Live Demonstration: The Governance Cockpit & Compliance Monitoring**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Walkthrough of the unified React-based Governance Dashboard.
    *   Demonstration of real-time G-SRI and key telemetry monitoring.
    *   Review of the OSCAL-based compliance matrix for the EU AI Act and DORA.
*   **Presenter:** Demo Lead

---

**(30-50 min) Live Demonstration: The Cryptographic Audit Trail**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Demonstration of a live governance event: a minor anomaly is triggered.
    *   Tracing the event from detection to a PQC-signed, immutable WORM log entry.
    *   Showcasing the system's automated record-keeping capabilities.
*   **Presenters:** Demo Lead, Tech Lead

---

**(50-70 min) Live Demonstration: Independent Supervisory Verification**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Walkthrough of the **Regulator Verifier Node**, a tool for independent oversight.
    *   Using the Verifier to cryptographically confirm the integrity of the audit record from the previous segment.
    *   Brief overview of the underlying TLA+ formal verification for the system's safety logic.
*   **Presenter:** Tech Lead

---

**(70-85 min) Live Demonstration: Automated Containment Drill**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Execution of a pre-planned `Red Dawn` stress test scenario.
    *   Demonstration of the system's automated containment protocols and failsafe mechanisms (OmegaActual).
    *   Showcasing the system's resilience and ability to maintain a safe operational state.
*   **Presenter:** Demo Lead

---

**(85-90 min) Open Discussion & Next Steps**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Q&A session.
    *   Discussion of next steps and the proposed cadence for sandbox engagement.
*   **Presenter:** Head of AI Governance
