# Project Sentinel: Onboarding Guide & Narrative Summary

## 1. Our Mission: Building Trust in AI

The Sentinel AI Governance Stack is a multi-layered, automated system designed to ensure our most critical AI models operate safely, fairly, and in compliance with global regulations. Its mission is to provide continuous, evidence-based trust in our AI infrastructure. What began as the "Supervisory Control Plane" (SCP) project has evolved into this production system.

## 2. The Journey: From Concept to Live Operations

The Sentinel stack was not built overnight. It was the result of a deliberate, multi-year, phased journey:

*   **Phase 1: The Sandbox:** We started with a simple idea: an automated control plane for a single AI model. We proved its viability in a controlled regulatory sandbox, establishing a transparent and collaborative relationship with our regulators from day one.

*   **Phase 2: The Live Pilot:** We moved the SCP into a live, pre-production environment. Over a year, we proved its core capabilities:
    *   **Policy-as-Code:** Enforcing rules automatically.
    *   **Circuit Breakers:** Halting the model when it became unsafe.
    *   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** Integrating cryptographic human sign-off for key decisions.
    *   **ZK-Fairness Proofs:** Using Zero-Knowledge proofs to verify model fairness without revealing sensitive data.

*   **Phase 3: Federation (The GIEN):** We expanded from a single system to a network. The Governance Incident Exchange Network (GIEN) was created to allow multiple institutions to share AI threat intelligence securely and in real-time, creating a collective defense.

*   **Phase 4: Hardening (The HDL):** In response to intelligence about a sophisticated nation-state threat ("Hydra"), we launched Project Medusa to build the Hydra Defense Layer (HDL). The HDL's **Perseus Engine** uses advanced ZK-analytics to detect coordinated attacks across the entire GIEN network. Its success was validated in a series of wargames.

*   **Phase 5: Production:** After successfully passing all tests, the complete, multi-layered system was activated in the live production environment.

## 3. The Current State: Operational & Vigilant

The system, now named **Sentinel AI Governance Stack v2.4**, is fully operational.

*   **It is LIVE:** It actively governs our critical AI models.
*   **It is MONITORED:** Performance is tracked via the `Daily DevSecOps & Regulatory Compliance Report`.
*   **It is STRATEGIC:** Its long-term effectiveness is managed by the `HDL Quarterly Strategic Review`, a forum that includes industry leaders and national security partners.

## 4. Key Lessons & Core Principles

The success of this project has codified our institution's core principles for AI Governance:

1.  **Govern-by-Design is the Gold Standard:** We build governance in from the start; we don't bolt it on later.
2.  **Automation is Non-Negotiable:** For AI at scale, manual oversight is impossible. Real-time, automated control is mandatory.
3.  **Proactive Regulatory Partnership Accelerates Innovation:** We treat regulators as partners to de-risk projects and move faster.
4.  **Real-World Validation is Irreplaceable:** The system proved its ultimate worth when it successfully handled a real production incident.
