## 1.2 The arc as a design argument

The arc is monotone in three properties, and this monotonicity is the design lesson:

1. **Verification decentralizes** — from a single suite run (VI-δ) to mutually attesting lattice segments (IX–X), then re-centralizes deliberately into one final root (XI).
2. **Trust assumptions weaken** — each phase must tolerate the failure of assumptions the prior phase relied on (clock sync, link liveness, shared constitutional text, eventually the existence of any counterparty).
3. **The record outlives the system** — from Phase IX onward the record architecture is explicitly designed to be readable and verifiable by parties who never participated in the system, culminating in Phase XI where the *only* remaining artifact is the record.

A governance record architecture designed against these constraints degrades gracefully at every real-world analog: institutional wind-down, regulatory-regime change, jurisdiction exit, acquirer due-diligence, archive-only custody.

Three rules carry from the operational phase into every declarative phase, and they are the exportable design content:

## 3.2 The invariant-preservation theorem (design form) ▽

Each transition is admissible only if it comes with a mapping proof: every invariant of the prior constitutional form is either (a) preserved verbatim, (b) strengthened, or (c) explicitly retired with a recorded retirement rationale sealed into the lattice. Silent invariant loss is the constitutional analog of an unlogged schema migration — forbidden by construction. # SECTION 4 — ACCESSION & PARTITION-TOLERANCE MECHANICS

## 4.1 GML Partition-Tolerance Specification ▽

The galactic mesh lattice assumes partition as the *normal* state, not the exception. Core rules:

1. **Every lattice segment is constitutionally complete** — a partitioned segment retains a full tripod locally (quorum, assurance, text) and continues sealing daily roots.
2. **Heal-time reconciliation is WORM-append, never rewrite** — on partition heal, divergent segment histories are cross-linked into the DAG with both branches preserved (IMTA TII constraint B, generalized).
3. **Authority never travels faster than attestation** — no segment may act on another segment's governance decisions before verifying its lineage chain (constraint D ceremony).
4. **Divergent constitutional text is a hard stop** (TV class): partitions that drifted textually cannot auto-merge; only human-quorum treaty process (E4) can reconcile.

## 4.2 Galactic Accession Codex ▽

Accession of a new governance domain to the lattice requires six sequential gates: (G1) constitutional-text hash equality or ratified delta; (G2) invariant-register adoption with mapping proof; (G3) crypto-lineage compatibility attestation; (G4) hardware-attestation estate registration; (G5) probationary dual-sealing period (candidate seals into both its legacy chain and the lattice); (G6) quorum ratification. Real-world analog: subsidiary onboarding into group-level model-risk governance, or a new jurisdiction entering the supervisory-college ring.

- **Inter-Civilizational Treaty Engine (ICTE)** — anchor `0x40262918bae3979431e3d0f8b685b784719e70b080fe2f7adb1d4626f6c22194`: machine-checkable treaty objects (parties, invariant intersections, evidence-exchange schedules, dissolution clauses) sealed as lattice nodes; every treaty carries its own CJCM-analog matrix between party constitutions.
