## 1. Mission & Purpose

The mission of the HDL Quarterly Strategic Review (QSR) is to provide a recurring, high-level forum for the GIEN Operating Committee to assess the effectiveness of the Hydra Defense Layer and the overall security posture of the Governance Incident Exchange Network. 

While the `HDL_Monthly_Performance_Report` focuses on tactical, operational health, the QSR is a strategic exercise. Its purpose is to move beyond immediate threats and analyze longer-term trends, sophisticated adversary evolution, and the strategic allocation of resources for the GIEN's defense.

## 2. Scope & Objectives

Each QSR will be a half-day, in-person meeting of the full GIEN Operating Committee and invited national security liaisons. 

**Key Objectives:**

1.  **Review of Meta-Trends:** Analyze the aggregated data from the three preceding monthly reports to identify subtle, long-term patterns that may not be visible in a single monthly snapshot. This includes changes in alert velocity, the emergence of new low-and-slow attack patterns, and shifts in the geographic or sector-based distribution of anomalies.

2.  **Adversary Doctrine Assessment:** Review any new intelligence regarding the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) of known threat actors (e.g., "Hydra"). The goal is to assess whether the HDL's current configuration and ZK-circuits are sufficient to defend against the *next* generation of attacks, not just the last one.

3.  **Red Team & Wargame Planning:** Commission and review the results of internal Red Team exercises and multi-institution wargames. The QSR will serve as the primary body for approving the scenarios and scope of these exercises.

4.  **Strategic Resource Allocation:** Based on the findings, make binding decisions on the GIEN's strategic direction. This may include:
    *   Authorizing the development of new ZK-circuits (`circuit_v2`, `circuit_v3`, etc.) to counter new threats.
    *   Commissioning fundamental research into new cryptographic or analytical techniques.
    *   Recommending changes to the underlying GIEN charter or protocols.

## 3. Inputs & Outputs

**Required Inputs for each QSR:**
*   The three (3) most recent `HDL_Monthly_Performance_Report` documents.
*   A summary of all significant correlation events detected during the quarter.
*   A formal threat intelligence update from national security partners.
*   The after-action report from the most recent wargame or Red Team exercise.

**Mandatory Outputs from each QSR:**
*   A formal set of meeting minutes, documenting all decisions and action items.
*   A **Quarterly Strategic Guidance** document, which will be disseminated to the GIEN Technical Working Group. This document will outline the committee's priorities for the upcoming quarter.
*   The approved charter and scenario for the next scheduled wargame or Red Team exercise.

## 4. Standing Agenda

1.  Review of Action Items from Previous QSR
2.  Threat Intelligence Update & Adversary Doctrine Review
3.  Quarterly Performance & Trend Analysis
4.  Wargame / Red Team Debrief
5.  Strategic Guidance & Next Quarter's Priorities
6.  New Business
