# 90-Minute Supervisory Roundtable: Facilitator's Guide

**Topic:** The Future of AI Assurance: From Process Audits to Governance Integrity
**Objective:** To move from passive listening to active engagement, generating supervisor buy-in and identifying key concerns regarding next-generation AI assurance.

---

### **Setup (Pre-Meeting)**
*   **Room:** U-shaped table to encourage discussion.
*   **Tech:** Projector for the 10-slide deck, whiteboard/flip chart for capturing discussion points.
*   **Materials:** Printed copies of the slide deck for all attendees.

---

### **Part 1: Setting the Stage (15 Minutes)**

*   **(0-5 mins) Introduction & Welcome:**
    *   **Facilitator:** "Welcome everyone. The purpose of this roundtable is to look beyond our current supervisory practices and begin planning for the challenges and opportunities of AI in the 2026-2035 timeframe. Today is about collaborative thinking, not a formal policy discussion."
    *   **Action:** Present **Slide 1 (Title)** and **Slide 2 (Agenda)**.

*   **(5-15 mins) The Current Landscape (A Shared Understanding):**
    *   **Facilitator:** "Let's start by grounding ourselves in the present. The last few years have been about maturing our 'Process Assurance' capabilities."
    *   **Action:** Present **Slide 3 (Where We Are Today)** and **Slide 4 (The Emerging Gap)**.
    *   **Facilitator:** "We've made great strides with frameworks like NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001. We've become very good at auditing an institution's *processes*. The core question for this group is: Is that enough for the complex, adaptive AI systems that are coming?"

### **Part 2: Introducing the Core Concepts (20 Minutes)**

*   **(15-25 mins) Framing the Problem: Governance Drift:**
    *   **Facilitator:** "We need a term for the problem we're trying to solve. We propose the concept of 'Governance Drift.'"
    *   **Action:** Present **Slide 5 (Governance Drift)**.
    *   **Facilitator:** "This is the delta between the system we *approved* and the system that is *operating*. It can be subtle, non-malicious, and nearly impossible to find with traditional audits. Does this concept resonate with the challenges you're seeing or anticipating?"
    *   **Note:** Pause here for 1-2 minutes to let the concept sink in.

*   **(25-35 mins) Proposing the Solution: Governance-State Attestation (GSA):**
    *   **Facilitator:** "If the problem is a diverging *state*, the solution must be to continuously verify that state. This is the idea behind Governance-State Attestation."
    *   **Action:** Present **Slide 6 (New Paradigm)** and **Slide 7 (How It Works)**.
    *   **Facilitator:** "The core idea is simple: we move from manually auditing paperwork to automatically verifying a cryptographic proof of integrity. This is a fundamental shift in supervisory technology."
    *   **Action:** Present **Slide 8 (Benefits)**.

### **Part 3: Open Discussion & Strategic Dialogue (45 Minutes)**

*   **(35-40 mins) Seeding the Discussion:**
    *   **Facilitator:** "This brings us to the core of our workshop. We've presented a high-level concept. Now, we need your expertise to ground it in reality. We'll use these four questions to guide our conversation."
    *   **Action:** Present **Slide 9 (Discussion Questions)**.

*   **(40-80 mins) Facilitated Group Discussion:**
    *   **Action:** Go to the whiteboard. Write down the four questions as headers.
    *   **Facilitator:** "Let's start with question one: What are the biggest blind spots you see today?" (Go around the room).
    *   **Facilitator:** As the discussion flows, guide it through the other questions. Focus on capturing key points on the whiteboard. 
    *   **Key Topics to Probe:**
        *   **Governance Drift:** Is this real? Have they seen examples? How would they currently even look for it?
        *   **Systemic Coordination Risks:** If one firm's AI governance drifts, how could it affect others? What happens if interacting AI agents from different firms create a systemic issue?
        *   **Supervisory Evolution:** What skills would supervisors need to manage this? What new regulations or guidance would be required?
        *   **Executive-Level Discussion:** How would you explain this to your leadership? What would they see as the biggest benefit or biggest risk?

*   **(80-85 mins) Synthesizing the Discussion:**
    *   **Facilitator:** "This has been an incredibly productive discussion. Let me try to summarize the key themes I've heard..." (Review the points on the whiteboard).

### **Part 4: Closing (10 Minutes)**

*   **(85-90 mins) Next Steps:**
    *   **Facilitator:** "We are at the very beginning of a long but important journey."
    *   **Action:** Present **Slide 10 (Next Steps)**.
    *   **Facilitator:** "Based on this conversation, we will be recommending the formation of a cross-institutional working group to explore these concepts further. Thank you for your time and invaluable contributions today."
