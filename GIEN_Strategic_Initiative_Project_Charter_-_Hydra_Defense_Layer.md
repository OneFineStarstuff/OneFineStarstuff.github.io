# Project Charter: GIEN Strategic Initiative

**Project Name:** Project Medusa - The Hydra Defense Layer
**Project ID:** GIEN-SI-2029-001
**Classification:** TOP SECRET // FOR COMMITTEE EYES ONLY
**Date:** [Date, ~1 Year after GIEN Production Go-Live]

## 1. Mission & Threat Summary

**Mission:** To design, develop, and deploy a next-generation "Hydra Defense Layer" (HDL) for the Governance Incident Exchange Network (GIEN). The HDL will provide a secondary, meta-analytical layer of security designed to detect and neutralize coordinated, multi-vector attacks by sophisticated state-level actors against the federated network itself.

**Threat:** Recent, credible intelligence from national security partners has identified a state-sponsored threat actor, codenamed "Hydra," actively developing capabilities to attack the GIEN. Hydra's suspected objectives are:
1.  **Cascading Failure:** Trigger a false positive in one institution that propagates through the GIEN, causing multiple institutions to unnecessarily contain their models, leading to economic disruption.
2.  **Trust Erosion:** Subtly manipulate GIEN messages to reduce their reliability, forcing institutions to distrust the network and fall back to manual, slower defense postures.
3.  **Coordinated Strike:** Launch a simultaneous, subtly different attack against multiple member institutions, designed to evade the GIEN's current generalization logic.

## 2. Project Scope & Key Deliverables

This project will build a new set of capabilities on top of the existing, proven GIEN infrastructure. It will not replace the current system but augment it.

**In Scope:**
*   **Cross-Network Anomaly Detection:** Develop a new ZK-powered analytics engine that analyzes patterns of alerts *across the entire network* to identify statistically improbable correlations that could indicate a coordinated attack.
*   **"Liar Detection" Protocol:** Implement a second-layer verification system where a subset of GIEN members can be called upon to independently and cryptographically attest to the validity of a suspicious alert, making it exponentially harder to spoof a credible threat.
*   **Controlled Wargaming:** Execute a series of live, multi-institution "wargame" drills to test the HDL against simulated Hydra attack patterns.

**Key Deliverables:**
1.  **HDL Technical Specification:** A detailed blueprint for the ZK analytics engine and the Liar Detection protocol.
2.  **Prototype Deployment:** A functional HDL prototype deployed in a sandboxed partition of the production GIEN.
3.  **Wargame After-Action Reports:** A series of reports detailing the performance of the HDL against the simulated attacks.
4.  **Production Deployment Proposal:** A final proposal to roll out the HDL across the entire live GIEN.

## 3. Governance & Timeline

*   **Oversight:** This project will be managed directly by a sub-committee of the GIEN Operating Committee, with mandatory participation from national cyber-defense liaisons.
*   **Timeline:** This is an emergency initiative. The goal is to move from charter to a functional prototype in 90 days.

**Conclusion:** The GIEN was built to defend against market chaos. Now, we must evolve it to defend against a thinking, malicious adversary. The work of Project Medusa begins immediately.
