**Objective:** To introduce the concept of Governance-State Attestation as the next logical step beyond infrastructure assurance, building a case for its adoption as a new standard of care for supervising systemic AI risk.

## Slide 2: The Core Problem: Governance Drift

*   **Slide Geometry:** 50/50 split. Left side has a simple diagram, right side has text.
*   **Banner:** The Challenge: Why Infrastructure Assurance is No Longer Enough
*   **Left Side (Diagram):**
    *   A box labeled "Approved Governance" with an arrow pointing to a slightly distorted box labeled "Operational Governance."
    *   The space between is labeled "**Governance Drift**."
    *   Sub-bullets below diagram: "Unauthorized Changes," "Model Decay," "Emergent Behavior," "Misaligned Agents."
*   **Right Side (Text):**
    *   **Executive Callout (Large Text):** "How do you prove your AI's rulebook is the same one it's actually using?"
    *   **Body:** Traditional assurance verifies the container (the infrastructure), but AI requires us to verify the *contents* (the governance logic). This gap, or "Governance Drift," is a primary source of systemic AI risk.
*   **Presenter Notes:**
    *   "The core problem is 'Governance Drift.' This is the gradual, often invisible, divergence between the governance we designed and approved, and how the AI system actually operates in the wild. It's not a hardware failure; it's a logic failure. Our current tools are not designed to detect it."

## Slide 3: The Solution: Governance-State Attestation

*   **Slide Geometry:** Dominated by a central diagram.
*   **Banner:** The Solution: Cryptographic Verification of Governance
*   **Central Diagram (The Governance Stack):** A 3-layer stack.
    *   **Top Layer:** **Policy State** (Rules, Constraints, Objectives)
    *   **Middle Layer:** **Model State** (Weights, Parameters, Routing Logic)
    *   **Bottom Layer:** **Infrastructure State** (Compute, TEEs, vTPMs)
    *   A cryptographic hash symbol (`#`) is shown summarizing the entire stack into a single "State Hash."
*   **Executive Callout (Right of Diagram):** "We can now create a cryptographic 'fingerprint' of our entire AI governance state."
*   **Message Bar (Bottom):** This moves beyond trusting process to verifying state.
*   **Presenter Notes:**
    *   "The solution is to treat governance itself as a machine-readable, verifiable object. We take a snapshot of the entire governance stack—from the high-level rules down to the model parameters—and generate a single, immutable 'State Hash.' This hash is our proof of integrity."

## Slide 4: The Supervisory Evolution Timeline

*   **Slide Geometry:** A horizontal timeline across the bottom of the slide.
*   **Banner:** Our Journey Towards Verifiable AI Governance
*   **Supervisory Focus Timeline (Graphic):**
    *   **Past (2015-2022): Infrastructure Assurance**
        *   *Focus:* Server integrity, network security, physical access.
        *   *Method:* Penetration testing, configuration audits.
    *   **Present (2023-2025): Process Assurance**
        *   *Focus:* Model validation, development lifecycle (SR 11-7), code review.
        *   *Method:* Process audits, documentation review (NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001).
    *   **Future (2026+): Governance-State Attestation**
        *   *Focus:* Verifying the live, operational governance state against the approved state.
        *   *Method:* Continuous cryptographic attestation, automated evidence generation.
*   **Executive Callout (Above timeline):** "We are leading the transition from *reviewing processes* to *verifying outcomes*."
*   **Presenter Notes:**
    *   "This isn't happening overnight. It's the logical third step in a supervisory journey we've all been on. We mastered infrastructure assurance. We're currently maturing process assurance with frameworks like the NIST RMF. Governance-State Attestation is the next frontier, and it's essential for managing advanced AI."
