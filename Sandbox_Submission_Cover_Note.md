Please accept this letter and the accompanying dossier as our formal application for entry into the 2026–2028 AI Governance Sandbox Program.

Our submission, the **Supervisory Control Plane (SCP)**, represents a novel, end-to-end system designed to provide provably safe, transparent, and verifiable governance for high-risk AI systems in systemically important financial institutions. The SCP is the culmination of extensive research and development in areas including zero-trust architectures, confidential computing, formal verification, and post-quantum cryptography.

Our objective for this sandbox is to partner with your office to collaboratively test, refine, and validate a new paradigm of automated, regulator-ready supervision. We aim to demonstrate a system that not only meets but exceeds the principles and requirements outlined in emerging global standards such as the EU AI Act, DORA, and NIST AI RMF.

As detailed in the attached dossier, our goals for Phase 1 of the sandbox are to demonstrate:
1.  **Immutable Auditing**: The successful operation of our PQC-WORM (Post-Quantum Cryptography, Write-Once-Read-Many) logging system for creating unforgeable audit trails.
2.  **Independent Verification**: The ability for a supervisory body to independently and cryptographically verify the integrity of our governance records without accessing sensitive institutional data.
3.  **Formal Safety**: The successful application of TLA+ formal methods to guarantee core safety and containment invariants.

The enclosed dossier contains the following key documents:

1.  **`Supervisory_Control_Plane_Architecture.md`**: A comprehensive technical document detailing the system architecture, Governance State Machine (GSM), cryptographic evidence pipeline, and the TLA+ formal specifications for our `SentinelContainmentProtocol`.
2.  **`Phase_1_Demonstration_Agenda.md`**: An agenda outlining the 90-minute live demonstration we have prepared for our proposed Phase 1 entry, which will showcase the system’s core functionality.
