## **3. Proposed Plan for Phase II: "Scale & Intelligence" (2029-2031)**

### **Strategic Goals**

1.  **Scale the Framework:** Onboard the next 15 G-SIFIs and 50 Fortune 500 institutions into the Sentinel ecosystem.
2.  **Enhance Supervisory Intelligence:** Move from reactive monitoring to proactive, AI-driven supervision.
3.  **Introduce Privacy-Preserving Audits:** Enable deeper, more frequent cross-jurisdictional audits without compromising data confidentiality.

*   Successful onboarding of all target institutions by end of 2031.
*   Demonstrable reduction in the mean time to detect model drift by >50% via ASA.
*   Successful execution of the first cross-jurisdictional, zk-SNARK-based audit.

## **4. Forward-Looking Analysis & Emerging Risks**

As we look beyond Phase II, the primary emerging challenge is the governance of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Artificial Superintelligence (ASI). Our roadmap allocates specific research and development cycles in **Phase III (2032-2035)** to address these risks, including the development of advanced containment protocols and the integration with civilizational governance frameworks like ICGC/GASO.

We recommend establishing a joint research working group with our supervisory partners in Q1 2027 to begin formalizing the requirements for AGI/ASI governance.
