## **1. Executive Summary**

Over the past several months, we have completed the design, documentation, and successful sandbox stress-testing of the Sentinel AI Governance Architecture. The complete body of work—the **Sentinel Governance Corpus**—is now finalized and provides a comprehensive, verifiable framework for managing AI risk at an institutional and systemic level.

We have moved beyond theory. The architecture is stable, the operational runbooks are complete, and the supervisory tools have been demonstrated to be effective. The time for planning is over, and the time for execution is now.

This document formally proposes the immediate initiation of the **Phase 1 Production Pilot**, as outlined in the `Sentinel 2035: A Strategic & Technical Roadmap`. We are ready to move from the sandbox to a live, monitored production environment.

## **2. State of Readiness**

All prerequisite artifacts for the commencement of Phase 1 are complete and have been delivered to your offices. These include:

*   **The Sentinel AI Governance Monograph v3.0:** The complete intellectual and technical foundation for the architecture.
*   **The Sentinel 2035 Roadmap:** The multi-year strategic plan we are now ready to execute.
*   **Regulator-Facing Supervisory Documentation:** A detailed guide on how the architecture provides verifiable compliance and enables real-time supervision.
*   **Full Implementation & Operational Guides:** The detailed technical checklists and design patterns required for our engineering teams.

Our sandbox stress tests (detailed in the `Phase-III-Stress-Test-Results.md` document) have proven the system's resilience against component failure, large-scale threats, and sophisticated adversarial attacks.

## **3. Phase 1 Production Pilot: Scope & Objectives**

**Objective:** To establish the foundational governance mesh and achieve baseline automated compliance for a designated set of high-risk AI systems in a live production environment.

**Scope:**

1.  **Systems:** We will onboard three initial AI systems, starting with the `FX-Arbitrage-Model-v4.7`.
2.  **Infrastructure:** We will deploy the full **Sentinel AI Governance Stack v2.4** into a segregated, highly-monitored production Kubernetes cluster.
3.  **Supervisory Access:** We will provide the Supervisory College with a live, read-only feed from the **Supervisory Digital Twin (SDT)** for the three pilot systems, commencing Day 1.
4.  **Compliance Baseline:** The pilot will generate continuous, automated evidence to satisfy the core requirements of **NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, and the EU AI Act** for the selected systems.

## **4. Proposal**

We request formal sign-off from the Board and acknowledgement from the Supervisory College to **commence the Phase 1 Production Pilot effective 01 July 2026**.

Upon approval, we will execute the `Sentinel_Implementation_Checklists` for the first system and schedule the first **Monthly Checkpoint Call** for the first week of August, as per the engagement plan.

This is the critical step in transitioning AI governance from a theoretical, process-oriented discipline to a live, verifiable, and data-driven reality. We have built the tools and laid the foundation. We are ready to proceed.
