## 1. Executive Vision: A Decade of Verifiable Governance

This document outlines the strategic roadmap for the deployment, maturation, and planetary-scale federation of the Sentinel AI Governance architecture from 2026 to 2035. The end-state vision is a global financial system where the risk of AI is continuously monitored, automatically contained, and verifiably governed according to a globally-agreed constitutional framework.

For G-SIFIs and Fortune 500 institutions, this roadmap provides a clear path to achieving demonstrable, automated compliance with the complex web of global regulations. For supervisory bodies, it provides a blueprint for building a shared, real-time, and unmediated view of systemic risk.

## 2. The Three Phases of Implementation

The 10-year program is structured into three distinct, sequential phases.

### **Phase I: Foundation & Integration (2026 - 2028)**

*   **Goal:** Establish the foundational governance mesh and achieve baseline automated compliance within the initial cohort of G-SIFIs.
*   **Key Technical Milestones:**
    1.  **Deploy Core Stack:** Deploy Sentinel AI Governance Stack v2.4 and SCP v3.0 into production across all participating institutions.
    2.  **Establish SDT Baseline:** Activate Supervisory Digital Twins (SDTs) for all designated high-risk AI systems, providing regulators with initial, live, read-only governance feeds.
    3.  **Implement Compliance-as-Code:** Operationalize **OSCAL-to-OPA** policy mappings for core regulations (NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, DORA, EU AI Act). Achieve an automated audit trail for these key frameworks.
    4.  **Hardware & Infrastructure Attestation:** Enforce **TPM/TEE and vTPM attestation** for all nodes running critical AI workloads. Standardize on secure **Kubernetes/GitOps and Terraform-based** deployment patterns.
*   **Key Regulatory Outcome:** Move from periodic reporting to continuous, evidence-based compliance for the foundational layer of AI governance and IT risk regulations.

### **Phase II: Automation & Federation (2029 - 2031)**

*   **Goal:** Mature the system's autonomous capabilities and federate the defense and response mechanisms across the entire mesh.
*   **Key Technical Milestones:**
    1.  **Activate Autonomous Agents:** Deploy the **AutonomousSupervisoryAgent** in "monitor and recommend" mode. Begin building a performance and drift baseline before granting containment authority.
    2.  **Federate the GIEN:** Fully operationalize the Governance Incident Exchange Network (GIEN) for automated, cross-institutional threat sharing.
    3.  **Introduce Post-Quantum Cryptography:** Begin the transition of the **post-quantum WORM audit logging** system, migrating historical log hashes and implementing PQ-CRYSTALS-Dilithium for all new signature generations.
    4.  **Operationalize zkML:** Move **zk-SNARK/SnarkPack and zkML proof pipelines** from observation to active enforcement for select, high-risk models, enabling privacy-preserving integrity checks.
*   **Key Regulatory Outcome:** Demonstrate a federated, sector-wide defensive posture. Provide supervisors with tools (ASPE-Global advisories) for coordinated, mesh-wide risk mitigation, satisfying advanced requirements of SR 26-2 and MAS/HKMA FEAT.

### **Phase III: Planetary Scale & Resilience (2032 - 2035)**

*   **Goal:** Achieve full planetary-scale automation, implement the highest levels of resilience, and finalize the transition to a fully post-quantum secure ecosystem.
*   **Key Technical Milestones:**
    1.  **Launch Omni-Sentinel Mesh v4.0:** Upgrade the entire network to the v4.0 specification, enabling true planetary-scale federation and the activation of the Global Merkle Root for treaty-level verification.
    2.  **Activate Autonomous Containment:** Grant trained and vetted AutonomousSupervisoryAgents live containment authority over their designated AI systems.
    3.  **Deploy On-Chain Kill-Switch:** Operationalize the **on-chain kill-switch** mechanism. The PKS activation semantics will be encoded in a smart contract, requiring a quorum of supervisory bodies to trigger a containment event.
    4.  **Complete PQC Transition:** Ensure all cryptographic processes, including the on-chain mechanisms and TEE attestations, are fully resilient against quantum attack vectors.
*   **Key Regulatory Outcome:** Achieve the ultimate goal of the ICGC/GASO civilizational compute-governance frameworks: a verifiable, resilient, and cooperatively-governed ecosystem for critical AI. Provide a technical foundation for enforceable international treaties on AI safety.

## 3. Core Technical & Regulatory Pillars (2026-2035)

This roadmap is built on a set of consistent technical and regulatory pillars that will evolve in sophistication through each phase.

*   **Constitutional AI Governance:** The foundation will always be the **GIES specification**, enforced via **OPA/Rego** and mapped to regulations via **OSCAL**. This ensures that all automation is aligned with human-defined rules.
*   **Zero-Trust Architecture:** The principle of "never trust, always verify" is absolute, from **TPM/TEE attestations** at the hardware level to mTLS and SPIFFE/SPIRE at the network level.
*   **Verifiable Proofs:** The reliance on cryptographic proof will deepen, moving from basic attestations to **zkML proofs** for model integrity and ultimately to the **Global Merkle Root** for planetary state verification.
*   **Multi-Jurisdictional Alignment:** Continuous automated mapping and evidence generation against the full suite of regulations (**Basel, DORA, NIS2, EU AI Act, GDPR, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, SR 11-7, etc.**) is a core, non-negotiable service of the mesh.

This roadmap presents an ambitious but achievable path toward a future of stable, secure, and verifiably governed AI. It provides a structured plan for G-SIFIs to manage risk and for supervisors to ensure the safety of the global financial system in the age of artificial intelligence.
