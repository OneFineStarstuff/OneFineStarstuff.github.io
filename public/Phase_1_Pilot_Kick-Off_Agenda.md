# Sentinel Phase 1 Production Pilot: Kick-Off Meeting Agenda

**Date:** 01 July 2026
**Attendees:**
*   **Institution:** Head of AI Governance, AI Architecture Lead, DevSecOps Lead
*   **Regulator:** Lead Supervisor (Fintech Division), Supervisory Technical POCs

**Duration:** 60 Minutes

**Classification:** Supervisory Confidential

---

## **Agenda**

**Objective:** To formally commence the Sentinel Phase 1 Production Pilot, confirm the scope and objectives for the first 30 days, and establish the communication and reporting cadence for the program.

---

**(0-10 min) Welcome & Formal Commencement**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Welcome from the Head of AI Governance.
    *   Formal acknowledgement of the approved pilot program, transitioning from the "Future (2026+)" vision to live execution.
    *   Confirmation of the meeting's objectives.
*   **Lead:** Head of AI Governance

---

**(10-30 min) Review of Pilot Scope & Day 1 State**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Reiteration of the Pilot's scope: Onboarding of `FX-Arbitrage-Model-v4.7` into the production Sentinel v2.4 stack.
    *   **Live Demonstration:** A brief, live walkthrough of the regulator-facing **Supervisory Digital Twin (SDT)**, showing the initial state of the system as of this morning.
    *   Demonstrate the successful generation of the first `HardwareAttestation-v3` and `GovernanceStateAttestation-v1` evidence objects, now logged in the post-quantum WORM ledger.
*   **Lead:** AI Architecture Lead

---

**(30-50 min) 30-Day Roadmap & Supervisory Engagement Plan**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Outline the technical milestones for the next 30 days, including the onboarding of the next two designated AI models.
    *   Confirm the process for providing supervisors with secure, read-only access credentials to the SDT dashboard.
    *   Review the format and delivery schedule for the **Daily DevSecOps Operational Verification Report**.
    *   Agree on the standing date and time for the **Monthly Checkpoint Calls**.
*   **Lead:** DevSecOps Lead

---

**(50-60 min) Q&A and Confirmation of Next Steps**

*   **Topics:**
    *   Open floor for questions from all stakeholders.
    *   Confirm next immediate actions and owners.
    *   Adjourn meeting.
*   **Lead:** Head of AI Governance
