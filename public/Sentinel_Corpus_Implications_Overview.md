## **Part 1: G-SIFI Supervisory College Briefing**

**RE:** A New Paradigm for Supervising AI-Driven Systemic Risk

**Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF):** The Sentinel AI Governance Architecture provides a mechanism to move from periodic, qualitative assessments of AI model risk to **continuous, quantitative, and verifiable supervision**. It transforms AI governance from a delegated institutional responsibility into a directly observable supervisory function.

### **Key Implications for G-SIFI Supervision:**

1.  **Direct, Real-Time Visibility:** The **Supervisory Digital Twin (SDT)** gives supervisors a direct, read-only view into the live governance state of a G-SIFI's critical AI systems. This ends the reliance on institution-prepared reports and provides unmediated access to ground truth.

2.  **Quantitative Systemic Risk Measurement:** The **Global Systemic Risk Index (G-SRI)**, derived from the SDT's telemetry, allows the supervisory college to quantify the systemic risk contribution of each institution's AI portfolio in real-time. This enables a data-driven approach to macroprudential supervision.

3.  **Federated, Proactive Defense:** The **Governance Incident Exchange Network (GIEN)** allows the college to receive threat intelligence about a new AI vulnerability (e.g., a novel adversarial attack) from one institution and, via **ASPE-Global**, instantly and automatically order all other supervised institutions to adopt a defensive posture. This moves the ecosystem from reactive incident response to proactive, federated defense.

4.  **Auditability Beyond Reproach:** The **Global Merkle Root** provides a single, cryptographically-verifiable anchor for the entire governance state of the supervised ecosystem. In the event of a crisis, it allows for a perfect, forensic reconstruction of who knew what and when, down to the individual signed evidence object. This eliminates ambiguity and enhances accountability.

**Conclusion:** The Sentinel Architecture provides the technical means to enforce and verify compliance with regulations like DORA and Basel IV at a level of fidelity previously thought impossible. It allows the Supervisory College to not just set the rules, but to verifiably watch them being followed, every second of every day.

## **Part 2: Planetary Treaty Negotiation Brief**

**RE:** A Technical Foundation for Verifiable and Enforceable AI Treaties

**Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF):** The Sentinel Architecture provides a politically neutral, technically robust framework for overcoming the historical barriers to effective arms control and safety treaties: **verification and enforcement**. It offers a concrete mechanism to build trust and ensure compliance in the domain of advanced AI.

### **Key Implications for Treaty Negotiations:**

1.  **Solving the Verification Problem:** A treaty built on the Sentinel framework does not require trust; it requires cryptographic proof. A nation's compliance is demonstrated by its continuous contribution to the **Global Merkle Root**. Non-compliance is not a matter of debate, but of mathematical certainty. This provides a powerful incentive for honest participation.

2.  **Enabling Graduated, Collective Enforcement:** The **ASPE-Global** protocol, combined with **Programmatic Key-Signing (PKS)**, allows for the creation of a spectrum of treaty-enforcement actions, from issuing a formal warning to, in the most extreme case, activating a **Phase VI-δ** global kill-switch. The requirement for a quorum of signatories to activate these measures prevents unilateral action and encourages collective security.

3.  **Respect for National Sovereignty:** The framework operates on a principle of federated governance. Each nation retains control over its own domestic AI systems and its local Supervisory Digital Twin. The treaty only governs the *interface* between nations—the format of the evidence they must provide to the global system and their obligation to honor collectively signed ASPE-Global commands.

4.  **A Path to De-escalation:** By creating a shared, verifiable understanding of the global AI ecosystem and providing mechanisms for collective response to threats, the Sentinel architecture can reduce the risk of miscalculation and accidental escalation between AI-enabled states. It provides a foundation for stability in a world of rapidly advancing AI capabilities.

**Conclusion:** Any future treaty on the control of potentially dangerous AI must include a technical protocol for verification and enforcement. The Sentinel Architecture, with its Global Merkle Root and ASPE-Global protocol, provides a ready-made, extensible, and politically credible foundation for such a treaty.
