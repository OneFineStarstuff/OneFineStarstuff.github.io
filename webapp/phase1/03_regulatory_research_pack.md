# 03 — Regulatory Research Pack (WP-098 source corrections)

**As-of:** 2026-11-10. Informational only; legal applicability requires jurisdiction- and entity-specific review.

## EU AI Act
Use the EUR-Lex consolidated text dated 27 July 2026 and Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI) as primary sources. The Digital Omnibus is an enacted amending regulation, not a proposal. Check the amended article, annex and transitional provision for each system and actor. A financial-services use case is not automatically high-risk.
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng
- https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai

## U.S. model-risk guidance
Federal Reserve SR 26-2 was issued 17 April 2026 and supersedes SR 11-7 and SR 21-8. It is risk-based supervisory guidance, not itself a prescriptive enforceable standard; applicability is institution- and regulator-specific. Retain SR 11-7 for historical lineage only.
- https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm

## Singapore MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines
MAS published the Guidelines on 7 October 2026 and states they take effect on 7 October 2027. Track publication and effective dates separately and assess institution-specific scope.
- https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/guidelines/guidelines-on-artificial-intelligence-risk-management-for-financial-institutions

## GDPR and DORA
Use the official EUR-Lex texts. GDPR Article 22 is not a blanket prohibition on automated processing; Article 35 DPIA requirements are risk- and processing-dependent. DORA applies from 17 January 2025 to in-scope EU financial entities.
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj

## NIST, ISO and PQC
NIST AI RMF 1.0 and AI 600-1 are voluntary frameworks/profile references, not legislation. ISO/IEC 42001 is a management-system standard, not an EU regulation. NIST FIPS 203 specifies ML-KEM key encapsulation; NIST posted a 17 November 2025 planning note identifying an issue for future correction, so check current errata. FIPS 204 specifies ML-DSA and FIPS 205 specifies SLH-DSA.
- https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/203/final
- https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/204/final
- https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/205/final
- https://pages.nist.gov/OSCAL-Reference/models/v1.1.2/
