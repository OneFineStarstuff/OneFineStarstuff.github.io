## **1.0 Introduction: The Necessity of Global Cooperation**

While the Omni-Sentinel stack provides the technical means for verifiable AI governance, technology alone is insufficient. The existential risks outlined in our first report are global in nature and respect no borders. A malicious AI developed in one nation can affect all nations. A structural failure in a globally integrated AI network can cascade across the planet. 

Therefore, a robust, enforceable, and global governance framework is not an option; it is a prerequisite for long-term civilizational survival. This report details the proposed multi-layered framework that the Omni-Sentinel architecture is designed to support and enforce.

---

## **2.0 The Multi-Layered Governance Model**

Effective AI governance requires a nested model of responsibility and verification, where each layer provides the foundation for the next.

1.  **Layer 1: Enterprise & Organizational Governance:** This is the foundational layer, where individual organizations deploy stacks like Omni-Sentinel to manage their own AI systems. This includes adhering to standards like ISO 42001 and the NIST AI RMF.
2.  **Layer 2: National Regulation:** Sovereign nations implement regulations like the EU AI Act, setting the legal requirements for AI systems operating within their jurisdiction.
3.  **Layer 3: International Accords & Treaties:** A global consensus is formed through binding treaties that govern the development and use of the most powerful AI systems. This is the only layer capable of addressing global catastrophic risks.

## **3.0 Proposed International Governance Bodies & Treaties**

To manage the risks of AGI and ASI, we propose the establishment of two core international bodies, governed by a new set of binding treaties.

### **3.1 The International Cyber-Governance Commission (ICGC)**

*   **Mandate:** A treaty-based organization, analogous to the IAEA for nuclear power, responsible for setting safety standards, conducting inspections, and promoting the safe development of advanced AI.
*   **Enforcement Power:** The ICGC's mandate is given technical teeth by the **Global AI Safety & Oversight (GASO)** treaty.

### **3.2 The Global AI Safety & Oversight (GASO) Treaty**

*   **Core Principle:** Prohibits the development or deployment of AGI/ASI systems that are not registered with and continuously monitored by a federated global governance network.
*   **Technical Enforcement via Omni-Sentinel:**
    1.  **Mandatory Registration:** To be compliant with the GASO treaty, any AI system above a certain capability threshold *must* have its digital twin registered in a national, ICGC-audited Sentinel Mesh.
    2.  **Continuous Attestation:** The system must continuously provide zero-knowledge proofs of its compliance with ICGC safety standards (e.g., alignment invariants).
    3.  **Treaty-Circuit Diagrams:** The core prohibitions of the GASO treaty are encoded directly into **Treaty-Circuit Diagrams**. An AI physically *cannot* generate a valid proof for an action that violates the treaty, as the circuit would fail to solve. This transforms a legal agreement into a cryptographic reality.

### **3.3 Future-Era Treaties**

As civilization expands, this framework is designed to evolve:

*   **Civilizational Compute Accord (CGA-ID):** Will regulate the total compute power available to any single entity, preventing the rise of a dominant, potentially hostile superintelligence.
*   **Stelliferous Era Accords:** Will govern the use of stellar energy resources, ensuring the long-term, entropy-minimizing survival of a galactic federation.
