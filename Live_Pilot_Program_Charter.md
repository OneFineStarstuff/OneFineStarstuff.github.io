# Live Pilot Program Charter

## 1. Introduction

This charter outlines the goals, scope, and governance of the Supervisory Control Plane (SCP) Live Pilot Program. The program will involve the deployment of the SCP in a production-like environment with a real, non-critical AI model.

## 2. Goals

The primary goals of the Live Pilot Program are to:

*   Evaluate the SCP's performance and reliability in a real-world setting.
*   Gather feedback from the regulator on the SCP's usability and effectiveness.
*   Identify and address any technical or operational challenges before a full production deployment.

## 3. Scope

The pilot program will focus on the following:

*   **Model:** A non-critical AI model for a low-risk application.
*   **Environment:** A production-like environment that is isolated from other systems.
*   **Duration:** The pilot program will run for three months.

## 4. Governance

The pilot program will be governed by a joint steering committee composed of representatives from our organization and the regulator. The committee will meet on a weekly basis to review the program's progress and address any issues that may arise.
