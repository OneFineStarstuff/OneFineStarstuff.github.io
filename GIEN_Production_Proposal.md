# Proposal: Establish Permanent Governance Incident Exchange Network (GIEN)

**Document ID:** GIEN-PROD-PROP-001
**Date:** [Date, End of 3-Month Pilot]

## 1. Executive Summary

This document presents the formal proposal from the GIEN Federated Pilot working group to transition the pilot program into a permanent, production-level network. The pilot has been an outstanding success, meeting all of its technical and governance objectives ahead of schedule.

We have definitively proven that a secure, high-speed, and reliable network for sharing anonymized AI threat intelligence between G-SIFI institutions is not only possible but essential. The successful execution of all three threat-sharing drills demonstrated the network's ability to rapidly disseminate actionable intelligence about data drift, novel attack vectors, and complex recursive events. 

We, the undersigned representatives of the participating institutions and the lead regulator, have unanimous confidence in the system's value and readiness. We request formal approval to operationalize the GIEN, moving it from a pilot to a permanent, trusted component of our collective AI risk management infrastructure.

## 3. Proposal for Production GIEN

We propose the following to establish the GIEN as a permanent entity:

1.  **Transition to Production Environment:** The existing pilot infrastructure will be migrated to a production-grade, highly-available cloud environment, managed by a neutral third-party entity (as defined in the GIEN charter).
2.  **Formalize Governance:** The Joint Governance Committee will be formalized into a permanent GIEN Operating Committee with a binding charter and operational mandate.
3.  **Expand Membership:** Begin a phased onboarding process to expand the network to include all other consenting G-SIFI institutions over the next 24 months.
4.  **Continuous Improvement:** Establish a dedicated technical working group to manage the ongoing evolution of the SIP protocol and the GIEN agent software.

## 4. Conclusion & The New Standard of Care

The era of institutions facing novel AI threats in isolation is over. The GIEN pilot has proven that a collaborative, federated defense is the most effective and responsible path forward. Establishing a permanent GIEN will set a new global standard of care for managing systemic AI risk.

This represents the final and most ambitious goal of the original vision for a Supervisory Control Plane. It moves beyond securing a single institution to creating a network that protects the integrity of the entire system.

We request formal sign-off from all participating institutional boards to proceed with the establishment of the permanent Governance Incident Exchange Network.

**The final chapter is written. The federated system is ready.**
