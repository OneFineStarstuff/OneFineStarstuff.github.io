# The Sentinel Project: Regulatory Engagement Model

## 1. Introduction

This document codifies the structured, transparent, and iterative engagement model that was a critical success factor in the Sentinel project (formerly the SCP). This model was developed to foster a collaborative and trust-based relationship with our regulatory partners, de-risking innovation and accelerating the path to production.

## 2. Core Principles

*   **No Surprises:** Regulators should be partners, not auditors. They are kept informed at every stage.
*   **Show, Don't Just Tell:** Demonstrations of working technology are prioritized over theoretical presentations.
*   **Data-Driven Dialogue:** All discussions are anchored in concrete, pre-shared performance metrics.
*   **Iterative Progress:** The project is broken into distinct phases, with formal review and sign-off at each gate.

## 3. The Engagement Cycle

Our engagement process follows a repeatable cycle for each phase of the project.

### Step 1: The Demonstration
*   **Activity:** A live, hands-on demonstration of the new capabilities for the current project phase.
*   **Goal:** To provide concrete, tangible evidence of progress and functionality.

### Step 2: The Immediate Debrief
*   **Activity:** Within 24 hours of the demonstration, a high-level summary is sent to the regulatory lead.
*   **Source Document:** `24-Hour_Regulator_Debrief_Summary.md`
*   **Goal:** To reinforce key takeaways and provide an immediate point of reference, ensuring alignment.

### Step 3: The Formal Follow-Up
*   **Activity:** Within one week, a detailed technical package is delivered. This includes formal specifications (e.g., TLA+), security analyses (e.g., ZK proofs), and any other relevant technical documentation.
*   **Goal:** To provide the deep technical evidence required for formal review.

### Step 4: The Monthly Cadence
*   **Activity:** A recurring monthly checkpoint call.
*   **Source Document:** `Monthly_Checkpoint_Call_Agenda.md`
*   **Goal:** To maintain a continuous, open channel for communication.
*   **Agenda Items Include:**
    *   Review of the **Monthly Metrics Report**.
    *   Discussion of any open questions from the formal follow-up package.
    *   Confirmation of the plan for the next month.

### Step 5: The Phased Review & Iteration
*   **Activity:** At the conclusion of a multi-month phase, formally close out activities and present a plan for the subsequent phase.
*   **Source Document:** `Monthly_Metrics_Report_Month_3.md` (specifically, the "Plan for Next Reporting Period" section).
*   **Goal:** To treat the project as a series of well-defined stages, each with its own goals and success criteria, ensuring the project does not proceed to more complex stages without mastering the current ones.

This model was instrumental in building the trust required to move the Sentinel stack from a sandbox concept to a live, production system.
