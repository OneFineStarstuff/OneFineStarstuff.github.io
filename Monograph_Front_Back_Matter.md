## **Preface**

The integration of artificial intelligence into the core functions of Global Systemically Important Financial Institutions (G-SIFIs) represents a paradigm shift. With it comes unprecedented capability, but also unprecedented complexity and the potential for novel, fast-moving systemic risks. The traditional methods of governance, which rely on periodic, manual, and process-oriented audits, are fundamentally insufficient for this new reality.

We can no longer be content with *reviewing processes*; we must move to *verifying outcomes*. Live.

This monograph is born from that necessity. It is not a theoretical exploration but a practical, architectural blueprint for a new class of verifiable AI governance. It provides a formal, constitutional framework and a detailed implementation guide for building and supervising AI systems that are not only powerful but also provably safe, stable, and aligned with human values and global regulations.

This work is intended for a tripartite audience: for **regulators and supervisors**, it offers a new and powerful lens for oversight through the Supervisory Digital Twin; for **system architects and engineers**, it provides a concrete, buildable specification for next-generation governance; and for **boards and senior executives**, it offers a clear methodology for managing the profound risks and unlocking the immense promise of G-SIFI-scale AI.

This is the manual for the next generation of financial infrastructure.

## **Abstract**

This monograph specifies the Sentinel AI Governance Stack, a comprehensive ecosystem for the design, implementation, and supervision of high-risk AI systems. It introduces the Governance Integrity Ecosystem Specification (GIES), a four-layer constitutional framework (GIMM → GIAF → GEE → PMGF) that establishes formally verifiable invariants for AI governance. The core thesis is that trust in complex AI systems can only be achieved through continuous, cryptographic verification, not periodic, procedural audits. The monograph details the Canonical Reduction Chain, a method for tracing every supervisory judgment back to foundational GIES invariants, ensuring that all governance states are provably correct. This is operationalized through the Supervisory Digital Twin (SDT), a high-fidelity, regulator-facing replica of the live system’s governance state. We detail the architecture for systemic-risk telemetry, federated defense (GIEN), and a Planetary Meta-Governance Framework for multi-jurisdictional compliance. The entire framework is designed for verifiable alignment with key global regulations, including Basel III/IV, DORA, NIS2, and the EU AI Act, providing a complete, publication-grade, and regulator-ready standard for the G-SIFI AI era.

## **Informative Annexes**

### **Annex A: Glossary of Terms**

*   **GIES (Governance Integrity Ecosystem Specification):** The constitutional foundation document for the entire framework.
*   **GIMM (Governance Integrity Meta-Model):** Layer 1 of GIES. Defines the universal, axiomatic truths of the system.
*   **GIAF (Governance Integrity Assurance Framework):** Layer 2 of GIES. Translates axioms into architectural principles.
*   **GEE (Governance Execution Environment):** Layer 3 of GIES. The live operational environment where governance is enforced.
*   **PMGF (Planetary Meta-Governance Framework):** Layer 4 of GIES. The framework for global, cross-jurisdictional governance.
*   **SDT (Supervisory Digital Twin):** A real-time, verifiable, high-fidelity digital replica of the live system’s governance state, for supervisory use.
*   **GIEN (Governance Incident Exchange Network):** The federated network for sharing AI threat intelligence.
*   **SIP (Sentinel Intelligence Protocol):** The privacy-preserving protocol used for transmissions on the GIEN.
*   **G-SRI (Global Systemic Risk Index):** The primary metric used to measure real-time systemic risk across the federated mesh.
*   **Governance-State Attestation:** The master cryptographic proof, generated by the SDT, that attests to the live system's overall compliance with the GIES.
*   **WORM (Write-Once, Read-Many):** The immutable log format used for all evidence and telemetry.

### **Annex B: Evidence-Object Catalog**

*   **`GovernanceStateAttestation-v1`:** The root evidence object. A signed, top-level proof from the SDT attesting that all invariants currently hold for the live system.
*   **`MoEComplianceProof-v2`:** A zk-SNARK proof demonstrating that a Mixture-of-Experts model has made a decision that complies with a specific governance policy (e.g., fairness, explainability) without revealing the model’s IP.
*   **`HardwareAttestation-v3`:** A signed report from a TEE/vTPM attesting to the integrity of the hardware and boot-time software of a specific node.
*   **`PolicyDecisionLog-v1`:** A signed log entry from an OPA enforcement point recording a specific policy decision (allow/deny), the input, and the policy version used.
*   **`SIPacket-v3`:** The Sentinel Intelligence Protocol packet containing an anonymized, zero-knowledge threat signature for broadcast on the GIEN.

### **Annex C: Telemetry-Signal Catalog**

*   **`ExpertHealthSignal`:** A low-level signal from an individual expert in an MoE model reporting its current health and confidence score.
*   **`RouterDecisionSignal`:** A signal from the MoE router indicating which expert(s) were chosen for a given task and the weights assigned.
*   **`PolicyEvaluationRequest`:** A raw signal indicating that a component is requesting a policy decision from OPA.
*   **`ContainmentHeartbeat`:** A periodic signal from a contained component, indicating it is still safely isolated.
