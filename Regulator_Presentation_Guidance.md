# Guidance: Designing Regulator-Facing Governance Slides

## 1. Core Principle: "The One-Page Dashboard"

Regulators and senior executives are not interested in the raw data; they need to see the top-level status and understand the system's evolution at a glance. The goal is to build trust through radical transparency, presented simply. Your slide should function like a strategic dashboard, not a technical log.

---

## 2. Recommended One-Page Slide Structure

**Slide Title: Omni-Sentinel Governance Architecture: Status & Evolution**

*(Use a clean, corporate template. Logos in the corners.)*

--- 

**(Top Left Quadrant) The Governance Stack: Our Defense-in-Depth**

*Use a simple, layered diagram (like a pyramid or stacked boxes). Each layer should have a label. This visually represents the defense-in-depth architecture.*

*   **Layer 5: Regulatory & Legal Alignment** (Basel, AI Act, etc.)
*   **Layer 4: AI Management & Governance** (ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF)
*   **Layer 3: AI Agent Supervision** (SCP, Perseus, HDL)
*   **Layer 2: Secure AI Operations** (DevSecOps, Containment, MoE Routing)
*   **Layer 1: Secure & Attested Infrastructure** (vTPM/TEE, ZKP Comms)

*Next to the diagram, have a single, large green checkmark with the text: **"All Layers Verified & Nominal"**.*

--- 

**(Top Right Quadrant) Daily Operational Status**

*This section mirrors the top of the daily report but is simplified further. Use icons (✅, ⚠️, ❌).*

*   **Overall System Health:** ✅ GREEN
*   **Governance State Attestation:** ✅ VERIFIED
*   **AI Agent Stability:** ✅ NOMINAL
*   **Containment Posture:** ✅ ENGAGED

*Add a single sentence below: "Continuous, automated verification confirms all systems are operating securely and within design parameters."*

--- 

**(Bottom Half of Slide) The Supervisory Evolution Timeline**

*This is a simple, horizontal timeline graphic showing the past, present, and future. It demonstrates maturity and a forward-looking plan.*

*   **Phase 1 (2020-2024): Foundational Controls**
    *   *Text below:* "Rule-based SCP, Manual HDL, Centralized Logging"

*   **Phase 2 (2025-2029): Sentinel Stack Deployed [CURRENT]**
    *   *Text below:* "Perseus Anomaly Detection, GIEN with ZKP, Automated Audits, ISO 42001 Certified"

*   **Phase 3 (2030-2035): Future Vision - Federated Governance**
    *   *Text below:* "Federated SCP for cross-jurisdiction supervision, Post-Quantum Cryptography, Autonomous Remediation"

*A large arrow should point to **[CURRENT]** to orient the viewer.*

--- 

## 3. Key Takeaways for Presenters

*   **Start High, Dive Low:** Present the slide first, then be prepared to answer questions by referencing the detailed `Daily_DevSecOps_Report`.
*   **Visuals over Text:** The diagrams do the heavy lifting. The checkmarks provide the summary. The timeline builds confidence.
*   **Consistency is Key:** Using this exact format every time builds familiarity and trust with the regulatory audience. They know exactly where to look for the information they need.
