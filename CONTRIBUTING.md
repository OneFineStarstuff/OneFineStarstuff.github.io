# Contributing to the Sentinel Project

We welcome contributions to the Sentinel AI Governance Stack project. As this repository is primarily a narrative case study, the most valuable contributions will be in the form of analysis, extensions to the narrative, or identification of new themes and lessons learned.

## How to Contribute

1.  **Fork the repository.**
2.  **Create a new branch** for your contribution. (e.g., `git checkout -b feature/your-analysis`)
3.  **Make your changes.** This could be adding a new document, editing an existing one, or adding comments/analysis.
4.  **Submit a pull request** with a clear description of your contribution.

## Areas of Interest

*   **Alternative scenarios:** What other kinds of incidents could the SCP or HDL have faced?
*   **Technical deep dives:** Can you expand on the technical specifications for any of the components?
*   **Regulatory analysis:** How might this system interact with new or proposed AI regulations?

Thank you for your interest in contributing!
